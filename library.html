Current sources for verification

  • Oil balances and prices: EIA Short-Term Energy Outlook and Today in Energy; IEA Oil Market Report; OPEC Monthly Oil Market Report.
  • Refining margins and capacity: EIA Refinery Capacity Report and Petroleum Supply Monthly (US); IEA Oil Market Report refining section; company 10-K filings for reported margins.
  • Benchmarks and assessment rules: Platts and Argus methodology guides (e.g. Dated Brent, Dubai, product assessments); ICE and CME contract specifications.
  • Product specifications: ASTM D4814 (gasoline), D975 (diesel), D1655 (jet); EN 228 and EN 590 in Europe; IMO MARPOL Annex VI for marine fuel sulphur.
  • Power markets: PJM and ERCOT State of the Market reports (independent market monitors); FERC and ACER publications.
  • Crude pricing research: Oxford Institute for Energy Studies papers (e.g. Fattouh on crude pricing).
  • Gas and LNG: IGU World LNG Report; GIIGNL Annual Report; EIA Natural Gas Monthly and weekly storage report; ACER European gas market monitoring.
  • Risk and xVA rules: Basel Committee MAR (FRTB) and CRE52 (SA-CCR) standards; ISDA SIMM methodology; EMIR and CFTC margin rules.
