Capstone: origination case studies

The capstone puts the courses to work on five deals an energy originator is likely to meet. Each case starts with a client brief and works through the same steps: understand the exposure, choose a structure, price it with a working model, plan the hedge and the risk controls, and sketch the key terms. Every number comes from code. Each case ends with desk scenarios that test whether you would make the right call with a client on the phone.

The five cases

  • Refinery intermediation: working capital, inventory funding and fee building (Courses 2–4).
  • CCGT toll: spread-option valuation, constraint haircuts and the bid (Courses 5–7).
  • Storage lease: intrinsic and extrinsic value and how much to pay for each (Courses 6–7).
  • Solar virtual PPA: shape, basis and volume risk for a corporate buyer (Courses 5, 7, 9).
  • Project-financed terminal: throughput tariff, debt sizing and related-party governance (Courses 3, 4, 9).

How to use the cases

  • Read the brief and write your own answer before reading on.
  • Use each widget to test the sensitivities that decide the deal.
  • The term-sheet sketches are starting points, not templates. Real terms depend on the market, the counterparties and the lawyers.
  • Sources are the course books, cited where a claim rests on them. Deal facts are illustrative and marked as synthesis.

A note on dates

The cases are set in today's markets, but the books behind them range from 1999 to 2023. Each case's Dated box flags where practice has moved on: bank exits from physical commodities, renewables' effect on power shapes, storage regulation, the corporate PPA market and terminal economics in the energy transition.

Modules

  1. Refinery supply and offtake intermediation ≈ 75 min ·
    A refiner short of working capital asks a trader to supply crude and buy products. Structure, fund, hedge and price the intermediation fee.
  2. CCGT tolling agreement ≈ 75 min ·
    A plant owner offers a 1-year toll on a 500 MW CCGT. Value the option, haircut for operating constraints, set a bid and plan the hedge.
  3. Storage lease ≈ 70 min ·
    A gas utility invites bids for a one-year lease of 2 Bcf of storage. Value intrinsic and extrinsic, decide how much extrinsic to pay for, and plan operations.
  4. Solar PPA hedge: shape and basis ≈ 70 min ·
    A corporate buyer wants a 10-year virtual PPA with a solar farm. Quantify shape, basis and volume risk and design hedges.
  5. Project-financed terminal with throughput contract ≈ 75 min ·
    A trader and an infrastructure fund develop an oil products terminal. Set the throughput tariff, contracted share and debt to meet lenders and equity.
