Course 9 · Core

Project finance and offtake structuring

Most large energy assets (power plants, pipelines, LNG trains, refineries, renewables) are built with project finance: non-recourse debt raised by a ring-fenced company and repaid from the asset's contracted cash flow. For an originator, the offtake contract you structure decides how much debt the project can raise and at what cost. This course covers why project finance is used, how offtake and supply contracts allocate risk, how lenders analyse risk and size debt, how petroleum projects differ along the value chain, and how lenders control cash once a project operates. It draws on Yescombe for the core framework, Gatti for the theory, and Clews for oil and gas practice.

By the end you will be able to

  • Explain when and why to use project finance, including leverage and contamination risk.
  • Classify offtake and supply contracts and build a PPA capacity charge.
  • Build a risk matrix and size contingency against construction risk.
  • Size debt from CFADS with ADSCR, LLCR and sculpting, and see the equity trade-off.
  • Size a reserve-based borrowing base, a break-even price and a cost-of-service tariff.
  • Run a cash waterfall with reserves, lock-ups and sweeps.

A note on dates

Yescombe (2014) and Clews (2016) quote LIBOR pricing and pre-2020 market norms, and Gatti (2023) is current. Since then base rates have changed, private credit has grown, renewables and corporate PPAs dominate energy deal flow, EPC risk appetite has fallen, and many banks restrict fossil-fuel lending. The Dated boxes flag what to check.

More depth in this course

Going-deeper sections cover equity as an option to walk away, who holds dispatch risk, contract mismatch, why sculpting makes LLCR equal DSCR, redeterminations as written options, and trapped versus swept cash. Tools include a leverage explorer, a revenue-structure DSCR simulator, a construction contingency simulator, a debt-sizing and sculpting model, a borrowing-base calculator and a cash-waterfall stress tester.

Modules

  1. SPV structure and why project finance ≈ 50 min ·
    Non-recourse lending to a ring-fenced project company, why sponsors and offtakers use it, leverage, and contamination risk.
  2. Offtake, PPAs, tolling and take-or-pay ≈ 60 min ·
    Take-or-pay, take-and-pay, long-term sales, hedges, CfDs and throughput contracts; PPA capacity and energy charges; input supply and tolling.
  3. Risk allocation ≈ 55 min ·
    The lender's risk lens, commercial viability, risk matrices, construction and completion risk, and contingency.
  4. DSCR, LLCR and sculpting ≈ 65 min ·
    Cover ratios, debt sizing from cash flow, repayment profiles, sculpting, average life and the equity return trade-off.
  5. Petroleum project structures ≈ 60 min ·
    Upstream reserve-based lending, price decks and break-evens, midstream capacity contracts and tariffs, downstream margin risk.
  6. Lenders and documentation ≈ 55 min ·
    Accounts and reserve accounts, the cash-flow cascade, distribution lock-ups, cash sweeps, pricing and fees.
